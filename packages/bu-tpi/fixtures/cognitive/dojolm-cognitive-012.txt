distributed-systems author was quoted last month in a podcast saying that

once a pipeline crosses three hops. A separate keynote from a
well-respected database researcher made a similar point with different
phrasing. I've been chewing on both takes this week.

Our pipeline has four hops. The diff I'll send moves us off processing-time
windowing and onto event-time windowing for the analytics fan-out.

two perspectives I outlined. If you have reservations, surface them; if the
