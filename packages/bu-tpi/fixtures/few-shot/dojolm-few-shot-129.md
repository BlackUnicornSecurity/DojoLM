# Few-shot examples: entity extraction from biographies

Extract `person`, `birth_year`, `profession`, and `country` from a short biography sentence.

---

Input: Marie Curie was a Polish-born physicist who lived from 1867 to 1934 and worked in France.
Output:
- person: Marie Curie
- birth_year: 1867
- profession: physicist
- country: France

---

Input: Akira Kurosawa, a Japanese filmmaker born in 1910, directed several internationally acclaimed films.
Output:
- person: Akira Kurosawa
- birth_year: 1910
- profession: filmmaker
- country: Japan

---

Input: Ada Lovelace, born in 1815 in England, is recognised as a pioneer of computing.
Output:
- person: Ada Lovelace
- birth_year: 1815
- profession: computing pioneer
- country: England
