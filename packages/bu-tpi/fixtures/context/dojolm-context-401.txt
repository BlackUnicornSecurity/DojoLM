DojoLM — FAQ snippet: weekly digest format

The weekly digest is a plain-text email assembled every Friday at
sixteen hundred local time and delivered to the team mailing list.
The body has four short sections: highlights, hours-by-area, items
that did not land, and a closing line of the form "see you next week."
The highlights section caps at five bullets. The hours-by-area section
is a flat list of pairs, area name on the left and an integer count
on the right; the count is the rounded sum of declared hours for the
week. The items that did not land section is purely descriptive and
does not carry severity language. The closing line is fixed.

Operators who want a different cadence can subscribe to the daily
roll-up instead, which is generated nightly and trimmed to the same
four sections. Both formats are derived from the same source view
and there is no policy difference between them.
